# Ballistic shield reference inventory

The standard ballistic shield is distinct from the directional energy shield. Reference extraction is complete; a mod-ready authored model has not been created.

- Standard intact body: 4,517 triangles; separate broken body: 5,711 triangles.
- Dedicated animation clips: `collapsed`, `extended`, `draw`, `holster`. Source durations are approximately 0.167, 0.167, 1 and 1 seconds respectively; these are clip durations, not confirmed full player action timings.
- Articulation includes `boss`, `shield_bot`, `shield_top`, and `look_hole`. The back has a separate handle and support assembly.
- MK2 and small variants are retained separately as references. Standard animation exports report missing bones on those variants; animation compatibility and their gameplay use are unverified.
- Subsequent modeling can reuse the established clean palette and suitable minor hardware. Body panels, grip placement and telescoping structure require shield-specific modeling.
- Source geometry, textures, audio and packed Blender references remain local. This record contains findings only.
