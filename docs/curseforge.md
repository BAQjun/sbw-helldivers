# SBW Helldivers

Bring Helldivers-inspired stratagems to Minecraft with this add-on for **Superb Warfare**. Equip a beacon, enter a stratagem code, and throw it to call in a hellpod or an Eagle attack.

## Stratagems

- **Automated sentries:** Machine Gun, Gatling, Rocket, Autocannon, Mortar, EMS Mortar, Gas Mortar, Flame, and Laser Sentries.
- **Manned defenses:** HMG Emplacement, Anti-Tank Emplacement, and Grenadier Battlement with a deployable protective wall.
- **Minefields:** Anti-Personnel, Anti-Tank, Incendiary, and Gas Mines.
- **Shield Generator Relay:** A transparent energy shield that blocks incoming projectiles while allowing shots from inside to leave.
- **Eagle support:** Eagle Airstrike, Eagle Strafing Run, Eagle Cluster Bomb, Eagle 110mm Rocket Pods, and Eagle 500kg Bomb.

Stratagems include deployment animations, mechanical effects, and sound. Use **X** to mark a creature or location. Sentries prioritize eligible marked enemies; mortars can bombard a marked location.

## Getting started

1. Obtain a Stratagem Beacon from the Helldivers Stratagems creative tab.
2. **Sneak + right-click** to choose up to four stratagems.
3. Hold **Alt** and enter the displayed directional code using **WASD** or the **arrow keys**.
4. **Left-click** to throw the activated beacon.

Mounted weapons support aiming and firing. Administrators can also deploy players in hellpods with `/helldivers drop <player>` and control marking with `/helldivers marking true` or `/helldivers marking false`.

## Requirements

- **Minecraft Java 1.20.1** with **Forge 47.2.0 or later**.
- **Superb Warfare**: developed against **0.8.9.1 hotfix**. Use its all-in-one distribution; its bundled model libraries do not need separate installation.
- **Kotlin for Forge 4.11.0 or later**, using a version compatible with Minecraft 1.20.1.
- Install this add-on and its required dependencies on **both client and server** for multiplayer.

## Language and compatibility

English (US) and Simplified Chinese are supported.

Some TACZ 1.1.8-series projectile interactions are supported, including hits on sentries, walls, mines, and shields. This is partial compatibility; TACZ is not a dependency.
