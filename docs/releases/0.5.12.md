## 中文

- 新增轨道炮攻击：在信标周围30米内锁定最大生命值最高的可攻击目标，红光追踪约1.7秒后开炮，并播放原版锁定音效。
- 炮弹造成160直击与160爆炸伤害，爆炸半径2.5米；没有可攻击目标时轰击信标落点。
- 支持轨道炮搜索范围、锁定时间、伤害、爆炸范围及冷却配置，默认采用满升级冷却179.55秒。

## English

- Added Orbital Railcannon Strike: targets the attackable enemy with the highest maximum health within 30 m of the beacon, tracks it with a red beam, and fires after approximately 1.7 seconds with the original targeting audio.
- Deals 160 direct and 160 explosive damage within a 2.5 m blast radius. With no valid target, it fires at the beacon's landing point.
- Configurable targeting range, lock duration, damage, blast radii and cooldown. Default fully upgraded cooldown: 179.55 seconds.
