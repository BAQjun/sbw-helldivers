# 0.4.9

## 中文

- 降低毒气地雷和毒气迫击炮产生的小范围毒雾高度，让云团更低、更舒展，缓解细高尖顶的外观；飞鹰毒雾与白烟的高度保持不变。
- 优化炮台模型渲染：缓存共享顶点和法线，减少重复变换，合并骨骼之间的顶点提交，并跳过动画中已完全缩小隐藏的几何。
- 对核验过的全透明／不透明材质取消不必要的半透明面排序，减少炮台附近的渲染开销；资源包替换材质时保留原有渲染方式。
- 渲染优化同时适用于精细模型和低面数模型，保留现有模型外形、贴图、骨骼动画及光影兼容处理。

## English

- Lowered small gas clouds from Gas Mines and the Gas Mortar Sentry, giving them a broader, flatter silhouette instead of tall pointed mounds. Eagle gas clouds and white smoke retain their existing height.
- Optimized sentry rendering by caching shared positions and normals, reducing repeated transformations, batching vertex submissions across bones, and skipping geometry fully collapsed by animations.
- Removed unnecessary translucent face sorting for verified binary-alpha textures to reduce rendering overhead near sentries. Replacement textures from resource packs retain the original material path.
- Rendering optimizations apply to both detailed and low-polygon models while preserving their geometry, textures, skeletal animations, and shader compatibility.
