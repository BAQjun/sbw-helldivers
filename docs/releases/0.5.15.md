## 中文

- 修复末地轨道激光在驱逐舰前方中断的问题。
- 修复主世界云层错误遮挡前方轨道激光的问题，保留实际深度遮挡。
- 新增“战备爆炸破坏方块”配置开关，默认关闭，独立于 SBW 爆炸总开关，可由房主或管理员在游戏内调整。

## English

- Fixed orbital laser beams fading out before reaching the Super Destroyer in the End.
- Fixed Overworld clouds incorrectly covering orbital laser beams in front of them, while preserving depth-based occlusion.
- Added a “Stratagem explosion block damage” setting, disabled by default, independent of SBW’s global explosion setting, and configurable in-game by the host or server operators.
