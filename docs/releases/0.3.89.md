## 中文

修正战备倒计时和冷却完成音效。

- 抵达与冷却分别使用各自的原始音效组，最后 3、2、1 秒播放对应秒数的提示音。
- 指令箭头动画开始时立即播放原始冷却完成提示音，取消此前的提示音延迟。
- 战备抵达时淡出抵达提示音，避免尾音延续到冷却阶段。
- 多个飞鹰战备共用计时器时，避免相同的倒计时音效重复叠加。

## English

Corrected stratagem countdown and completion audio.

- Arrival and cooldown now use their respective original sound groups, with distinct cues for the final 3, 2 and 1 seconds.
- Plays the original cooldown completion cue when the input-arrow animation begins, without the previous notification delay.
- Fades out the incoming cue when a stratagem arrives, preventing its tail from lingering into cooldown.
- Prevents identical countdown cues from stacking when multiple Eagle stratagems share a timer.
