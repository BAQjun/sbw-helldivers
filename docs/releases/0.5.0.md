# 0.5.0

## 中文

- 新增超级驱逐舰呼叫、方位调整与撤离，三秒后跃迁并播放原版音效。
- 超级驱逐舰固定停留在世界中；超出支援范围或当前维度没有可用舰船时，轨道战备禁用并显示灰白图标。
- 新增轨道精准打击：直击 640，爆炸 240，内半径 4 米、外半径 12 米，炮弹从舰船所在方向飞向信标。
- 可配置舰船支援范围、高度、方位偏移及精准打击的抵达时间、伤害和爆炸范围。

- 修复“飞鹰”烟雾攻击和毒气空袭落弹音效缺失，补入对应原版爆开音效。
- 新增配置页面的战备自动收回开关，控制弹药耗尽或时限到达后的自动缩回。
- 左上角战备自动提示新增平滑进出动画。

## English

- Added Super Destroyer call-in, repositioning and withdrawal, with a three-second jump delay and original warp sounds.
- Super Destroyers remain anchored in the world. Orbital stratagems become unavailable and display gray-and-white icons when out of range or without a supporting ship in the current dimension.
- Added Orbital Precision Strike: 640 direct damage, 240 explosive damage, a 4 m inner radius and a 12 m outer radius. The shell approaches the beacon from the ship's direction.
- Added server settings for ship coverage, altitude and bearing offset, as well as strike arrival time, damage and blast radii.
- Restored original bomb burst sounds for Eagle Smoke Strike and Eagle Gas Airstrike.
- Added an in-game setting to disable automatic stratagem retraction after ammunition or operating time runs out.
- Added smooth entry and exit transitions to automatic stratagem HUD notifications.
