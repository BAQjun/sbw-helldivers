## 中文

- 修复“飞鹰”110mm 火箭巢与火箭哨戒炮尾迹因远距离实体同步延迟而缺失或断开的情况。
- 尾迹从发射开始沿实际飞行路径生成，并衔接至命中点，保留原有烟迹外观与光影兼容。

## English

- Fixed missing or disconnected trails from Eagle 110mm Rocket Pods and Rocket Sentries caused by delayed entity tracking at range.
- Trails now follow the actual flight path from launch to impact, preserving their original smoke appearance and shader compatibility.
