## 中文

- 持续型轨道战备开炮后显示“正在进行”倒计时，最后一轮炮弹结束后才显示“已抵达”。适用于加特林、空爆、120mm、380mm和游走火力网。
- 左上角战备状态与信标状态保持一致，原有冷却时间不变。
- 修正轨道120mm高爆弹火力网的图标，替换为原版三枚炮弹图案。

## English

- Sustained orbital stratagems now show an “In progress” countdown after firing begins and display “Arrived” only after the final ordnance finishes. Applies to Gatling, Airburst, 120mm, 380mm and Walking Barrages.
- The upper-left stratagem list and beacon label now reflect the same activity state without changing cooldown timing.
- Corrected the Orbital 120mm HE Barrage icon to its native three-shell design.
