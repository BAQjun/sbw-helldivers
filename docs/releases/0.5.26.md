## 中文

- 电磁区域释放时改用简短的原版爆炸特效。
- 蓝色雾气改用烟雾、毒雾同款的体积形态，改善平整的顶部与边缘。
- 轨道电磁冲击与电磁迫击炮的实际作用高度统一为 5 格，并同步调整电光分布。

## English

- Replaced the EMS release burst with a brief vanilla explosion effect.
- EMS blue fog now uses the same billowing volumetric shape as smoke and gas, improving its flat top and edges.
- Increased the effective height of Orbital EMS Strike and EMS Mortar Sentry fields to 5 blocks and aligned their electrical effects.
