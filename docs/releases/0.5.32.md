## 中文

- 修复 NeoForge 1.21.1 中战备爆炸的烟尘、烟迹和火球粒子在 Iris 光影下无法正常显示的问题。
- 恢复烟尘与光影半透明合成的兼容，并保留无光影时的柔边效果。

## English

- Fixed stratagem explosion dust, smoke trails, and fireball particles not rendering correctly with Iris shaders on NeoForge 1.21.1.
- Restored compatibility with shader-pack translucency while preserving soft edges with shaders disabled.
