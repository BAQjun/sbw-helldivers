# 0.4.11

## 中文

- 修复毒气地雷与毒气迫击炮的小范围毒雾实际可见高度不足 4 格的问题，为顶部渐隐保留额外空间。
- 小范围毒雾使用宽而圆润的顶部轮廓，避免抬高后形成尖锥。
- 保留雾气向下蔓延及根据实心方块限制绘制的行为。

## English

- Fixed small gas clouds from Gas Mines and the Gas Mortar Sentry appearing shorter than 4 blocks by allowing additional room for the upper fade.
- Small gas clouds now have a broad, rounded crown rather than a narrow peak.
- Retained downward fog spreading and solid-block terrain clipping.
