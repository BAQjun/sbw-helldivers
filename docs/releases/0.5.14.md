# 0.5.14

## 中文

- 新增轨道激光：持续移动照射敌人，每5刻造成25点伤害并持续施加3秒燃烧。
- 采用原版25秒持续时间、50米搜索半径和10米/秒光斑速度，加入原版开始、持续与结束音效及发光光束。
- 命中地面或生物时，在实际接触点播放短促高速的白热喷流与飞溅火星效果。
- 抵达后立即隐藏信标光柱并进入冷却，默认提供3次使用次数；支持配置照射、索敌、冷却和次数参数。
- 超级驱逐舰完成撤离并重新进场后，补满该玩家所有有限次数战备的使用次数，保留原有冷却；调整方位不触发补充。

## English

- Added Orbital Laser: continuously sweeps across enemies, dealing 25 damage every five ticks and refreshing three seconds of burning.
- Uses the native 25-second duration, 50 m search radius and 10 m/s impact speed, with original start, sustain and stop sounds and an emissive beam.
- Rapid, sharp white-hot impact jets and molten sparks follow the actual terrain or creature contact point.
- The beacon disappears and cooldown starts as soon as irradiation begins. Includes three uses by default and configurable irradiation, targeting, cooldown and charge settings.
- Fully withdrawing and recalling your Super Destroyer replenishes all of your limited-use stratagem charges while preserving existing cooldowns. Repositioning does not replenish charges.
