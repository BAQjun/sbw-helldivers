## 中文

- 新增游戏内“炮台无限弹药”开关，支持自动炮台与人员操作型炮位的弹药及火焰燃料；火箭保留装填，激光保留过热机制。
- 人员操作型战备不再因工作时限到达而自动收回，仅在弹药耗尽且玩家离开后收回，仍遵循“战备自动收回”开关。
- 完善模组列表搜索，支持中文名、模组 ID 及名称中的空格、下划线和连字符。

## English

- Added an in-game Unlimited turret ammunition option covering sentries, manned emplacements and flame fuel. Rocket reloads and laser overheating remain active.
- Manned stratagems no longer retract when their operating lifetime expires. They retract only after ammunition runs out and the operator dismounts, respecting the Automatic retraction setting.
- Expanded mod list search to support Chinese names, the mod ID, and names containing spaces, underscores or hyphens.
