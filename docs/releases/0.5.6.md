## 中文

- 新增轨道120mm高爆弹火力网、轨道380mm高爆弹火力网与轨道游走火力网，均为5轮、每轮3发。
- 120mm散布半径27米，单发560直击、200爆炸伤害，内／外半径3.3／10米；同轮间隔0.75秒，轮间停顿2秒。
- 380mm散布半径36米，与游走共用轨道精准打击炮弹；同轮间隔1.5秒，轮间停顿3秒。
- 游走沿投掷点至信标落点方向推进，参考解包数据采用3米/秒推进速度、25米散布半径。
- 添加对应原版指令、图标、名称和满升级冷却；轮数、间隔、散布与推进速度等参数可配置。

## English

- Added Orbital 120mm HE Barrage, Orbital 380mm HE Barrage and Orbital Walking Barrage, each firing five three-shell salvos.
- 120mm spreads over a 27 m radius and deals 560 direct / 200 blast damage with 3.3/10 m inner/outer radii; shots are 0.75 s apart with a 2 s pause between salvos.
- 380mm spreads over a 36 m radius and shares Precision Strike ammunition with Walking Barrage; shots are 1.5 s apart with a 3 s pause between salvos.
- Walking advances along the throw-to-beacon direction at 3 m/s with a 25 m spread radius, based on unpacked game parameters.
- Added native input codes, icons, names and fully upgraded cooldowns, plus configurable salvo counts, timing, spread and walking speed.
