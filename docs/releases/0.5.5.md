## 中文

- 新增轨道空爆攻击：4 轮，每轮间隔 4 秒；每枚母弹空爆后分裂为 12 枚子弹。
- 母弹与子弹均造成 50 直击、60 爆炸伤害；母弹内／外半径为 0.2／0.5 米，子弹为 3／6 米。
- 分裂散布参考原版解包参数，空爆高度与其他攻击参数可配置。
- 移除轨道加特林爆炸的屏幕震动；机炮炮台与火箭炮台的爆炸震动强度降低至原来的 35%。

## English

- Added Orbital Airburst Strike: four salvos, four seconds apart. Each carrier bursts into 12 bomblets.
- Carriers and bomblets deal 50 direct and 60 blast damage. Inner/outer radii are 0.2/0.5 m for carriers and 3/6 m for bomblets.
- Fragment spread follows unpacked game parameters; burst height and attack settings are configurable.
- Removed explosion screen shake from Orbital Gatling Barrage. Reduced Autocannon and Rocket Sentry explosion shake to 35% of its previous strength.
