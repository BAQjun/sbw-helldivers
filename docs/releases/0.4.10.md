# 0.4.10

## 中文

- 毒气地雷与毒气迫击炮的毒雾高度最低为 4 格。
- 区域雾气可向下扩散，底部使用自然起伏的体积轮廓，并根据附近实心方块限制扩散，适应台阶与坑洼，减少地下无用绘制。
- 继续优化精细与低面数模型：移除完全遮挡的内部面和退化三角形，合并同色共面的小方块表面，保留可见轮廓、曲面精度、贴图与骨骼动画。
- 空投仓落地后，跳过被完整实心方块包住的模型部分；挖开地面即可重新显示。下落与阴影绘制保留完整模型。

## English

- Gas clouds from Gas Mines and the Gas Mortar Sentry now have a minimum height of 4 blocks.
- Area fog can spread downward with a billowing underside. A block-based terrain mask limits its descent around ledges and depressions and avoids unnecessary underground rendering.
- Further optimized both detailed and low-polygon models by removing fully hidden interior faces and degenerate triangles, and merging coplanar, uniformly colored cuboid surfaces. Visible silhouettes, curved-surface detail, textures, and skeletal animations are preserved.
- Landed Hellpods skip geometry fully enclosed by solid blocks. Digging away the ground restores the retained geometry. Falling pods and shadow rendering retain the complete model.
