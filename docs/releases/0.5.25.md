## 中文

- 参考原版演示重做电磁区域：以悬浮的蓝白电光、短促细电弧和轻薄蓝雾表现放电，并加入短暂的命中闪光。
- 轨道电磁冲击波攻击与电磁迫击炮共用新效果，兼容普通渲染与光影。
- 为轨道电磁接入独立的原版命中、持续放电与结束音效；修正电磁迫击炮区域展开音效的播放延迟。

## English

- Reworked EMS fields around the original preview: suspended blue-white sparks, brief fine arcs, a light blue haze and a short impact flash.
- Orbital EMS Strike and the EMS Mortar Sentry share the new effects, supporting both vanilla rendering and shader packs.
- Added distinct native impact, electrical ambience and shutdown audio for Orbital EMS Strike; corrected the EMS Mortar field activation sound delay.
