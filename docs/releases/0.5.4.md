## 中文

- 新增轨道加特林火力网：5 轮共 300 发连续扫射，落点围绕信标游走，普通弹与高爆弹按 3:1 混装。
- 直击伤害 30；高爆弹额外造成 60 爆炸伤害，内半径 1.5 米、外半径 4.5 米。新增对应配置、原版图标和扫射音效。
- 超级驱逐舰调整方位改为直接从当前舰位跃迁到目标位置。
- 红色战备取消 3×3 部署空间限制。

## English

- Added Orbital Gatling Barrage: 300 continuous rounds over five salvos, wandering around the beacon, with a 3:1 regular-to-HE mix.
- Each round deals 30 direct damage; HE adds 60 blast damage with 1.5 m inner / 4.5 m outer radii. Includes configurable settings, the original icon and barrage audio.
- Super Destroyer repositioning now warps directly from its current anchor to the destination.
- Removed the 3×3 deployment-clearance requirement for red stratagems.
