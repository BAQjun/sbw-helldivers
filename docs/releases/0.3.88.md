## 中文

新增左上角战备自动提示。

- 激活、投出及正在抵达的战备会显示在对应的战备槽位置，切换到其他物品后仍会显示。
- 战备抵达后继续显示冷却倒计时 3 秒，并在冷却剩余最后 3 秒时再次弹出。
- 冷却结束后，指令箭头依次出现；全部显示后停留 1 秒再收回。
- 为倒计时和战备就绪添加独立提示音，抵达与冷却计时音在剩余 3、2、1 秒时播放。
- 修正剩余使用次数的数字在白框内的居中位置。

## English

Added automatic stratagem notifications in the upper-left corner.

- Shows activated, thrown and incoming stratagems at their corresponding loadout positions, including when switching away from the beacon.
- Keeps the cooldown countdown visible for 3 seconds after arrival, then brings it back for the final 3 seconds.
- Reveals the input arrows in sequence when cooldown finishes, holds the completed sequence for 1 second, then closes the notification.
- Adds distinct UI audio for countdown ticks and stratagem readiness, with countdown pulses synchronized to the final 3, 2 and 1 seconds of arrival and cooldown.
- Centers remaining-use digits inside their white boxes.
