## 中文

- 新增轨道毒气攻击、轨道电磁冲击波攻击与轨道烟雾攻击，使用原版图标、指令与中英文名称。
- 三种炮弹均造成50点直击伤害。毒气和电磁各投放一枚；烟雾同时投放六枚，在信标周围15米内散布。
- 按解包记录设置区域：毒气15米/15秒、电磁13米/15秒、每枚烟雾12米/30秒。沿用体积区域效果、毒气混乱、电磁眩晕与烟雾遮挡索敌机制。
- 新增独立的伤害、弹量、散布、区域范围与时长配置；支持冷却及存档恢复。

## English

- Added Orbital Gas Strike, Orbital EMS Strike and Orbital Smoke Strike with native icons, input codes and Chinese/English names.
- All shells deal 50 direct damage. Gas and EMS each fire one shell; Smoke fires six simultaneously within 15 m of the beacon.
- Field settings follow decoded data: Gas 15 m/15 s, EMS 13 m/15 s and each Smoke field 12 m/30 s. Reuses volumetric effects, gas confusion, EMS stun and smoke visibility occlusion.
- Added independent damage, shell count, spread, field radius and duration settings, with cooldown and save/load support.
