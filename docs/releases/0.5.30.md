## 中文

- 修复 NeoForge 1.21.1 中反人员、燃烧、毒气和反坦克地雷的发光材质不显示的问题。
- 修正发光层随视角转动时的位置与深度遮挡，适用于普通渲染、Iris 光影和两种模型质量。

## English

- Fixed missing emissive surfaces on Anti-Personnel, Incendiary, Gas, and Anti-Tank Mines on NeoForge 1.21.1.
- Corrected camera transforms and depth alignment for mine emission, supporting standard rendering, Iris shaders, and both model quality settings.
