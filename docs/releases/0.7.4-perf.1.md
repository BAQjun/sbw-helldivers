## 中文

- 用已修正的减面模型替换十种炮台、五种护卫犬及其背包、生物处理器、精炼厂、战术终端和设备部署信标的现有模型。
- 保留主要轮廓、可见结构、标识、动画和武器挂点，减少细小凹槽与重复曲面的几何开销。
- 无损重排近景炮台贴图图集，减少未使用区域与重复像素；远距离模型使用独立的轻量图集，保持贴图坐标兼容。
- 本版本为性能测试版，主要收集近距离观察、多设备同屏和光影环境下的表现反馈。

## English

- Replace existing models for ten sentries, five Guard Dogs and their backpacks, the bioprocessor, refinery, tactical terminal and equipment deployment beacon with the revised reduced-polygon models.
- Preserve the main silhouettes, visible structures, markings, animations and weapon attachment points while reducing geometry used by small recesses and repeated curved surfaces.
- Losslessly repack close-range sentry texture atlases to reduce unused regions and duplicated pixels. Distant models use separate lightweight atlases with compatible UV layouts.
- This performance test build focuses on close-range viewing, multiple deployed devices and shader-enabled gameplay.
