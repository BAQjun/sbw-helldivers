## 中文

- 新增轨道凝固汽油弹火力网：6轮、每轮5发，弹间隔0.5秒、轮间停顿2秒，散布半径25米。
- 炮弹造成80直击与60爆炸伤害，内外半径3.3/10米；留下半径10米、持续21秒的重型燃烧地火。
- 沿用兼顾中心覆盖与同轮爆炸间距的散布规则，支持战备冷却、持续状态和存档恢复，并提供伤害、时序、范围与地火配置。

## English

- Added Orbital Napalm Barrage: six five-shell salvos, 0.5 s between shells, a 2 s pause between salvos, and a 25 m spread radius.
- Shells deal 80 direct plus 60 blast damage with 3.3/10 m inner/outer radii, leaving heavy-burning ground fire within 10 m for 21 seconds.
- Uses center-covering, separated salvo patterns, with cooldowns, ongoing-strike status, save persistence, and configurable damage, timing, spread and ground fire.
