## 中文

- 为反人员、燃烧、毒气和反坦克地雷添加兼容光影的发光部位。
- 优化地雷渲染：空投仓开盖后才显示挂载地雷，共用渲染缓存，并按观察距离减少模型细节。
- 优化已落地地雷的地面支撑检测，保留接触触发、连锁引爆和支撑移除后的下落行为。
- 修正反坦克地雷中央骷髅标志，并同步投弹器上的挂载地雷材质。
- 为空投仓落地添加短促、随距离衰减的镜头震动，也适用于玩家空投视角。

## English

- Added shader-compatible emissive surfaces to anti-personnel, incendiary, gas, and anti-tank mines.
- Optimized mine rendering with shared geometry caches and distance-based detail levels. Mounted mines are displayed only after the hellpod cover opens.
- Optimized ground-support checks for settled mines while preserving contact triggers, chain reactions, and falling when support is removed.
- Corrected the central skull marking on anti-tank mines, including those mounted on their dispenser.
- Added brief, distance-based camera shake on hellpod impact, including the player deployment view.
